Frequently asked questions

How long did it take you to make the timeline?

Approximately 200 hours from May 2024 to February 2025. Parts of the timeline are from cctvcctvcctv's Old Timeline. Those 9 months was primarily spent updating the timeline from 2021 to 2026 where cctv’s stopped being updated.

How much of the Timeline did you do?

75% of it, cctvcctvcctv's old timeline was very basic, only containing featured tracks, major contests and certain events. Due to forum corruption and character limits, the timeline was moved to a Google Document on the 6th of November 2025, and ever since the 15th of February 2026, the timeline is now hosted on a much cleaner and visually appealing GitHub website.

What's the difference between this and the Old Timeline?

Years from 2021 onwards are now populated with dates; the old timeline stopped being updated mid-2021. Every major game update is included in this version. A significantly larger number of contests are provided and include dates of every round/result, which the old timeline didn't. A comprehensive list of significant threads has been added. Ghosting-related dates have now been added; the old timeline had nothing about Official Featured Ghosts, Ghosting Awards, achievements, etc. Trackmaking and ghosting statistics added for relevant dates (feature number, earning of a medal or title, etc.). And just overall, a wider range of important events/dates from every year.

What inspired you to make this Timeline?

I absolutely love researching stuff and learning about the history of many subjects, one of them being video games. Free Rider HD is a unique game, and its story/progression should be told in full depth, as well as providing information for the community that they may find helpful. I wanted to make something to leave my mark on this game forever, and I feel this timeline does that. The legacy of the old timeline should live, and I strive to remake its glory, but on a much wider scale.

When will you stop updating the Timeline?

Never! I'm not leaving this game anytime soon. I love this community, and I want to always provide something for them, one of the things being this timeline. I also enjoy the process of making something this big.

Besides adding new things for the current year, what's next for the Timeline?

Not much; I have completed 95% of what I set out to do from day 1 of making this timeline. The remaining 5% involves extra trackmaking and ghosting statistics, finding dates for more significant events from years ago and adding more historical contests.
