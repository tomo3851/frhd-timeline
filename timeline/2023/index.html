  • 04/05

    TheMemeBiker miraculously earns the "Secret trophy", earning 500 trophy points and becoming a Forum Legend (500+ trophy points) out of nowhere, being top 5 in points on the forums. Conversation here. According to t12, the way the trophy point was intended to be handed out broke and was somehow given to TheMemeBiker. His birthday was on the same day he got the trophy, which could have played a factor in why he got the trophy. This prompted Totoca12 to delete the trophy, and to this day, we do not know how one could obtain the trophy regularly. The only one who knows is Stig who added the trophy on the 18th of June, 2018.

ShamatoZ is banned from forums and Official Fetaured Ghosts thread.

    11/05: Totoca12 makes another update about removing ShamatoZ from Official Ghosting Awards thread and updating monthly awards for the new winners and top 3 that ShamatoZ was initially part of.

    13/05: Totoca12 updates the thread again about dealing with previous cheaters on the FRHD All-Time Top 100 Ghosters List by leaving them on previous lists but putting an asterisk and different colours for unverified ghosters or those caught cheating. Totoca12 goes through the bans on each FRHD-related site and the moderator team settles with a FRHD Community Forums ban for ShamatoZ. The Grace Period is proposed by NotVeryGood to allow anyone in the ghosting community to admit to cheating and owning alt accounts for less strict consequences and dealing with big problems in the ghosting scene faster. Totoca12 makes one last announcement on providing video proof from ghosters who have held at least one featured ghost at some point in time so less suspicion is put on people in the ghosting scene. All official ghosting threads are unlocked on the 15th of May.

  • 29/08

    Super Mario World by TPlacella featured, their 1st featured track, entry for Super Mario Maker Contest, counted as one feature. TPlacella earns Recognised Trackmaker medal for publishing 1 featured track ((dantexpress)) The track was initially over 10,000 kb, making TPlacella the 3rd person in FRHD history to exceed the track size limit. TPlacella was unable to decrease the track size due to problems in loading the track and publishing. Elibloodthirst stepped in to help and reduced the overall track code to 9,800 kb. But when Eli posted the track on TPlacella's account, the second half of the track had all the physics lines disappear. Eli and TPlacella fiddled with the code for a couple of days but concluded that it was corrupt somewhere in the middle and impossible to fix. Eli proceeded to split the track in two at the wishes of TPlacella and they were both published a day apart. Both tracks have the featured track ribbon on them but only counts as one feature for TPlacella. (comments by TPlacella)

    • Super Mario World. 1 (track published 20/08/2023) 5368 kb

    • Super Mario World. 2 (track published 21/08/2023) 4535 kb
