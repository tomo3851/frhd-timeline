  • 06/05

    After 11 years, 5 months and 29 days since Free Rider HD was released, and exactly 11 years to the day of the release of the editor, FRHD reaches the magical 1,000,000 Track ID, but not without a little controversy. IAmMutskie, Protvod and Sltg28 used alt accounts to bring the Track ID count from 999,990 - 999,999. Just as Sltg28 was about to publish the 1M Community Collab to turn the Track ID over to 7 digits, erikeriksson2001 swoops in (with a suspected bot) and takes the spot with Million track follow the link, only 6 seconds after Track ID 999,999 was published. People were outraged that a track like that could arguably take the most sought-after ID number in the history of the game.
