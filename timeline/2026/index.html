  • 24-30/03

    24/03: Free Rider Toolkit released by Calculus, separating web-based tools from Free Rider Lite.

    An extended toolset for FRHD, designed to enhance the web experience with powerful features that aren’t available by default. While the base game has limitations around account and content management, this extension fills those gaps by giving users more control and flexibility directly within the site.

    Key features
    • Account management tools — update your email and manage account-related settings
    • Content control — remove your own tracks and races with ease
    • Additional utilities — streamlined tools to improve overall usability and workflow

    30/03: Calculus comes up with a solution for broken avatars on forums, removing avatar uploads from UI to remove confusion since they no longer work. Calculus also enables a feature that had been unintentionally disabled, showing a notification counter on the tab icon.

  • 21-25/10

    21/10: Calculus reports he has been in contact with Kano, and they are willing to fix the forums and possibly update the game. Free Rider HD Community Forums are down for maintenance due to a PHP vulnerability. A large button saying “Join the Discord” appears when you try to load the forums. An influx of members joins the FRHD Discord Server, curious about what is going on, pushing the number of members close to 1000. A feedback channel is made so the community can make a “laundry list” after pawflix posts his lengthy list of changes the game needs on the Discord Server.

    21/10: FRHD Mobile Version 3.3.0 released. With performance improvements and bug fixes, it is the first update to the game since the 24th of October 2018, almost 8 years.

    25/10: FRHD Mobile Version 3.4.1 released. An option to delete your mobile account appears in-game.

  • 25/10

    OpenRider by tomiy with contributions from Pie42, Calculus, Polygon, Ness and pinn released, a TypeScript rewrite of the JavaScript game on top of the Canvade engine. A Canvas Rider feel clone with new powerups such as launcher, mud and command block, as well as a new feature called Status Effect that includes invincibility, slippery, no steer, engine off, heavy, low gravity, mirror and turbo. The editor includes debugs, layers and a circle tool, and there is a daily endurance mode to see how fast and far you can go on a generated track.

    For a full breakdown and overview of the game, go here. For previous versions of OpenRider, go here and here

    26/10: Co-founder and current President of Kano Eric Haight (Eric) starts talking in the Discord Server under the name Jackson, expressing how the team is working on updates for the Mobile and Web versions of FRHD, also confirming the team will let the community know what will happen with the forum.

  • 28/10

    Seemingly out of nowhere, Charlie Bodman (Char), a former Kano and FRHD Developer who is the most important figure in the game’s history, rejoins the Discord Server. He discusses how he met up with Eric and brings up the topic of shutting down the forums. He also reveals that he has a 2-year-old daughter and that his wife, Lucy Fennell, is expecting again in February 2027! Interestingly, he states that Eric once asked him if he wanted to work on FRHD again. Char pitched Free Rider Community Edition (FRCE), a side project of his before he left Kano, but nothing eventuated. Currently, Char is working on a game with a similar canvas feel to Free Rider called Drawlandia. With support for real-time collaboration, it involves a small character traversing drawn worlds. Teaser videos here, here and here.

    With talks about creating an Official FRHD Discord Server run by Kano, Elibloodthirst, the current owner of the server, makes a comment that he won’t relinquish the server and that the community want some control. Although the future of the “unofficial” server remains unknown, Eric discusses how the new server would look and operate here and here. For the time being, the button saying “Join the Discord” is removed from the Free Rider HD Community Forums page that is down for maintenance.
