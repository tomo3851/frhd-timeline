    24/03: Free Rider Toolkit released by Calculus, separating web-based tools from Free Rider Lite.

    An extended toolset for FRHD, designed to enhance the web experience with powerful features that aren’t available by default. While the base game has limitations around account and content management, this extension fills those gaps by giving users more control and flexibility directly within the site.

    Key features
    • Account management tools — update your email and manage account-related settings
    • Content control — remove your own tracks and races with ease
    • Additional utilities — streamlined tools to improve overall usability and workflow

    30/03: Calculus comes up with a solution for broken avatars on forums, removing avatar uploads from UI to remove confusion since they no longer work. Calculus also enables a feature that had been unintentionally disabled, showing a notification counter on the tab icon.
